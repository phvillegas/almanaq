# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant].

The short version: be decent. Assume the person on the other side is trying to help,
say what you mean, and keep criticism about the work rather than the person.

Behaviour that will not be tolerated: harassment, personal attacks, and publishing
somebody's private information.

## Enforcement

Report anything through GitHub, by opening an issue or by contacting the maintainer
directly. Reports are handled by the maintainer, who may edit, hide or delete comments
and block accounts.

One person maintains this project, so expect a reply in days rather than hours.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
